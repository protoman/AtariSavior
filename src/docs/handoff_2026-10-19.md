## Task 1 (do this first) — flip the snake's initial direction at load

**Why:** the authored `dir` should mean *where the snake will come out*, and the
snake's first movement should be the retreat into its wall. Today the first move
follows the authored dir, so a snake placed facing outward comes out before it
retreats. Workaround until it lands: place the snake facing INTO the wall.

**Where:** `src/bank2.asm`, inside the room-load body (`LoadEnemyRam`, called from
`EnterRoomB2` — `#22` moved it there). It walks records via `EnemyOffTable`, copies
x (+1) and y (+2) into `EnemyRamX/Y`, then reads the dir at +3 (`$FF` = left,
`0` = right) and ORs the per-slot bit into `EnemyRamD`.

**Change:** for a SNAKE (record type 2, offset +0) invert that sense — OR the bit
when the record says left, clear it when it says right. ~6-10 B in bank2's free
space. **No kernel change**: the patrol bounds no longer read the dir at all, so
this load-time store is the only consumer of it.

**Verify:** add an assert to `tools/test_enemy_movement.py` that the snake's live
dir is the inverse of the record's, then a Stella look — place a snake facing open
air; its first move must be INTO the wall. (Geometry note: with a one-tile wall an
8 px snake can never be *fully* hidden at its placement; it is fully inside only at
the inner end, and only if the wall continues behind it.)
