            Posted July 8, 2013 (edited)

            I love RockBox, especially because it supports OGG Vorbis. For those who aren't familiar, OGG Vorbis is an open-source, patent-free alternative to MP3, and it happens to sound better than MP3 when using the same bitrate.

             

            RockBox has a lot of neat toys bundled with it, but if all you're worried about is music playback, I would suggest getting an iPod Video, also known as the 5th generation iPod ("5.5 generation" for the slightly redesigned model). Depending on which model you get, you get a hard-drive based iPod with up to 80GB capacity, more than enough for any good music collection. The RockBox installer works painlessly, and will set up the iPod to be dual-boot, meaning you get to keep the original Apple firmware as well (good for watching videos, since RockBox doesn't support video on the iPod Video very well; too much proprietary hardware).

             

            Avoid getting what's currently being sold as the "iPod Classic," also known as the 6th generation iPod. Apple completely redid the innards. The RockBox team has made great strides getting it to work, but installation is not a very user-friendly experience. 5th generation iPods are easily found on eBay.

             

            RockBox is also available as an Android app, so I use it on my phone as well. Unfortunately the Android app is still very crash prone, so it's not quite ready for prime time.
